## Research Summary

Luke Chang's lab investigates the **neurobiological and psychological mechanisms underlying emotion and social interactions**. Core questions include:

- How are emotions and expectations represented in the brain?
- How do emotions and expectations influence decision-making and learning in social contexts?
- How do we learn about others' mental states?
- How is information transmitted between people?

### Methods

The lab employs a combination of:
- **Naturalistic fMRI** — studying brain activity during ecologically valid social interactions
- **Computational modeling** — formalizing social and emotional processes
- **Representational similarity analysis**
- **Intersubject correlation** — comparing neural responses across individuals
