# An edited or rejected draft signs against the drafting whoever edited or rejected it

**Crates:** `mecha-core` (`src/appraisal.rs`, `src/outbox.rs`), `mecha-cli` (`commands/sessions.rs`)

## What is wrong

Appraisal reads the outbox as the `edit` channel: a staged draft released unchanged is a +1.0, an edited one a −1.0, a rejected message a −1.0. Outbox items carry stamps saying who acted: `resolved_by` (who released or rejected it) and `edited_by` (who edited it), each an `Option<closure::Actor>`, `None` on items from before the stamps existed.

The unchanged-release arm already respects those stamps: it signs only when the owner released it, and signs nothing otherwise. The edited and rejected arms do not look at them at all. So a run's own shell rejecting its own draft, or an edit that was not the owner's, still counts as the owner's −1.0 verdict against the drafting, and that verdict feeds the downstream readers that score rules.

## What fixed looks like

Apply the release's rule to edits and rejects, **going forward**, and keep the history as it is:

- **A rejected draft** signs −1.0 when it was rejected by the owner (`resolved_by` is `Some(Actor::Owner)`), or when it carries no stamp at all (`resolved_by` is `None`, from before the stamps). Rejected under any other stamp (`OwnerApproved`, `Unknown`, …) it signs nothing.
- **A sent, edited draft** signs −1.0 when it is the owner's own edit (`OutboxItem::owners_edit`), or when the edit itself is unstamped (`edited_by` is `None`) and the release is unstamped or the owner's. An edit stamped as anyone else's signs nothing, even when the owner released it.
- Everything else on this channel is unchanged: the unchanged-release arm, the outcome-report arm above it, the rule that a rejected publish is bookkeeping rather than a verdict, and pending items signing nothing.
- Put the predicate in one named place on `OutboxItem` that appraisal consults, rather than spelling it out inside the match.
- The `sessions appraise` readout's line for rejections with a reason that is not the owner's own words should also say that a stamped one signs nothing.

An existing appraisal test asserts that an edit released under an `OwnerApproved` stamp still signs −1.0. That is the old behaviour; update it.
